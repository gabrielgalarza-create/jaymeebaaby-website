Party FAQ

Quick answers.

How much space and power do you need?
A 6' table's worth of footprint and two standard outlets on separate circuits for most parties. Larger setups need more — she'll confirm once she knows the venue.
Can guests request songs during the party?
Yes, and she'll filter them against your do-not-play list. If you'd rather nobody requests anything, that's fine too — just say so up front.
Do you do Quinceañeras and debuts?
Regularly. Court entrances, waltz, surprise dance, changing of shoes — she'll rehearse the cues with your choreographer and run the whole program.
Can you play outdoors or in a backyard?
Yes, with power and a rain plan. She'll ask about noise ordinances and neighbors before quoting so there are no surprises at 10pm.
How late can you go?
Additional hours are billed at an hourly rate quoted up front. Most venues cap at 1am or 2am — she'll work to whatever your permit allows.
What if my party is in three weeks?
Send it. Short-notice dates are often open, especially weeknights and off-season. Worst case she'll refer you to a Bay Area DJ she'd book herself.
