Your day, hour by hour

What Jaymee is doing while you're getting married.

You should never have to look for your DJ. Here's the default run of show — yours gets customized on the planning call.

Start My Timeline  →

  1. 2 hours before

    Load in, set the ceremony system, sound check with your officiant, confirm processional cues with the coordinator.

  2. Ceremony

    Prelude, processional, vows amplified so the back row hears them, recessional on the beat.

  3. Cocktail hour

    Separate system in the cocktail space while the main room flips. Volume set for conversation, not competition.

  4. Reception

    Grand entrance, dinner sets, toasts, first dance, then the floor opens and doesn't close.

  5. Last call

    Final song, send-off cue, quiet breakdown after guests are gone. Your venue gets the room back on time.

Wedding FAQ

Ask before you book.

If your venue or planner needs anything specific, send it over — Jaymee has worked with most Bay Area coordinators.

Is the MC included or extra?
Included in every package. Grand entrance, toasts, first dance, parent dances, cake, bouquet, send-off. She'll confirm every name and pronunciation with you before the day.
Do you handle the ceremony too?
Yes, in The Signature and The Headline. That's a separate sound system in the ceremony space with a lapel mic for the officiant and a handheld for readings — so your vows are actually heard.
What about our do-not-play list?
Treated as absolute. You get a shared planning doc for must-plays, maybes and nevers. If Uncle Rob requests the song on your never list, the answer is no.
Can you take requests from guests?
Your call. Some couples want an open floor, others want requests routed through them or the planner. Both work — just tell her which on the planning call.
Our venue needs a certificate of insurance.
Standard. Send the venue's requirements and the COI is issued in their name at no extra cost, usually within two business days.
What does it take to hold our date?
A signed agreement and a 30% retainer. The date is released to other inquiries until both are in — she won't hold a date informally, because it isn't fair to the couple who's ready.
Do you do South Asian, Filipino or Latin weddings?
Yes, regularly. Jaymee is Bay Area born and raised and plays Latin, Filipino, Bollywood, Afrobeats and Top 40 fluently. Send your family's must-plays and she'll build around them.
Can we meet before we book?
Absolutely — a 20-minute video call, no charge, no pressure. Most couples book after it, but you're not signing anything on the call.
