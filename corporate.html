Corporate FAQ

Before you send the SOW.

Can you invoice with net terms and a PO?
Yes. Net-30 is standard for established companies, and PO numbers are referenced on every invoice. A 30% retainer holds the date for first-time clients; repeat clients can book on invoice alone.
Do you carry liability insurance?
$1M general liability. COIs with additional-insured endorsements are issued to your venue within one business day at no charge.
Can you keep the music clean?
Clean radio edits are the default for corporate work. If your crowd wants explicit versions after a certain hour, just say so — she'll switch on your cue, not before.
Will you MC the awards or program segment?
Included in Holiday Party & Gala and up. Speaker walk-ons, award announcements, raffles and housekeeping. She'll rehearse names and pronunciations with your event lead beforehand.
Our venue has house sound. Do we still need your system?
Often not — if the house system is adequate she'll patch into it and the quote drops accordingly. She'll confirm with your venue's tech contact before quoting.
Can you brand the experience?
Yes. Uplighting in your brand colors, custom audio stings for walk-ons, branded booth sets and social-ready content capture are all available in the Activation package.
How far in advance should we book December?
Bay Area holiday party season is effectively sold out by early September. If you're planning December, inquire by July.
