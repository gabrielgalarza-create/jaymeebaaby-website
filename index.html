Questions

The things everyone asks first.

Anything not covered here, just ask — it's a real reply from Jaymee, not a form response.

Ask A Question  →
How much does a DJ actually cost?
It depends on hours, guest count, whether you need ceremony sound, how much lighting the room needs and how far the travel is. Send those details and you'll get a firm number in writing within 24 hours — no call required first, and nothing is signed until you've seen the quote.
How far in advance should I book?
Peak Saturdays in May–October and all of December book 9–12 months out. Off-peak dates and weekdays are often available inside 90 days. If your date is close, send it anyway — cancellations happen.
Do you MC, or just play music?
Both, and it's included. Grand entrance, toasts, first dance, cake, bouquet, last call. Clear and warm, never the cheesy radio-voice thing. If you'd rather she stay quiet and just play, that's fine too.
What areas do you cover?
San Francisco, the East Bay, the Peninsula, San Jose, Marin, Napa, Sonoma and Santa Cruz with no travel fee. Tahoe, Monterey, Sacramento and destination events are quoted with travel included.
Can we pick the songs?
Yes. You get a shared playlist for must-plays and a do-not-play list that is treated as law. Beyond that, Jaymee reads the floor live — that's the part you're really paying for.
What if you get sick or something goes wrong?
Jaymee carries backup gear to every event and has a vetted network of Bay Area DJs on standby. In five-plus years she has never missed a booking, and your contract protects you either way.
Do you provide sound for the ceremony?
Yes — a separate ceremony system with wireless lapel and handheld mics, so vows and readings are heard in the back row. It's included in every wedding package.
