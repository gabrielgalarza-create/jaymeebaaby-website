Meet Jaymee

Bay Area born.
Bay Area booked.

Jaymee Gutierrez picked up the craft in 2021. Within a year she was a mixshow DJ on 99.7 NOW. Four years later she's played Outside Lands, Oracle Park and a national championship — and she still treats a 120-person wedding like the most important room she'll play that year.

Two names

Jaymeebaaby by night.
JayByDay by day.

Most DJs are one of two things: the club act who can't run a wedding timeline, or the wedding vendor whose "party set" is a Spotify playlist with transitions.

Jaymee runs both, deliberately. Jaymeebaaby is the name on the festival flyer and the Saturday-night mixshow — that's where the taste and the crowd-reading get sharpened every single week. JayByDay is that same instinct wearing a suit: timelines, coordinators, COIs, clean edits and a grandmother who needs to hear the toast.

You're hiring one person who has done both a thousand times. That's the whole pitch.

The sound

Genre-agnostic, crowd-obsessed.

Top 40, hip-hop, R&B throwbacks, EDM, house, Latin, reggaetón, Afrobeats and Filipino OPM — mixed live, not queued in blocks.

Growing up in the Bay means a room with four generations and six cultures in it isn't a challenge, it's Tuesday. That's why open format here means actually open, not "Top 40 with one salsa song at 11pm."
